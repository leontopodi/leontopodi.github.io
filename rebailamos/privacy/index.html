Privacy Policy

Effective August 18, 2026. ReBailamos keeps your recordings and imported practice media on your device while using the limited services described below.

What we collect

We do not receive your recordings, imported media, song names, YouTube video IDs, or YouTube viewing history. ReBailamos does not sell personal information, display advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

ReBailamos uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand coarse product activity, such as the type of practice source used, whether a capture or save completed, and purchase or feedback outcomes. These events do not contain media titles or identifiers, recordings, gesture frames, or feedback text. Advertising-personalization signals are disabled and the app does not use Apple’s advertising identifier.

Google may process technical information generated by the analytics service, such as app, device, and network information, under the Google Privacy Policy. Learn more about privacy and security in Firebase.

Camera, microphone, and media

With your permission, ReBailamos uses the camera and microphone to record your takes. You may also choose an audio file, a reference video, an Apple Music track, or a YouTube video for practice. Recordings, imported media, and practice settings are processed and stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

When you save a finished take, ReBailamos adds it to your Photos library. It remains there until you delete it.

YouTube API Services

If you choose the YouTube feature, ReBailamos uses YouTube API Services. Before YouTube loads, the app asks you to agree to this policy and provides links to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. By using the YouTube feature, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

If you use the in-app search, ReBailamos sends your search terms, together with the app’s bundle identifier and API credential, to the YouTube Data API and displays the returned video results. If you paste a link, the app extracts its video ID on your device. When you select a result or use a link, the app sends the video ID to the YouTube Data API to obtain the title and duration and to verify availability, embedded-playback permission, live status, and Made-for-Kids status. ReBailamos does not receive or retain your search terms.

The official embedded YouTube player then connects directly to YouTube. Google and YouTube may receive information such as your search terms, IP address, device and app information, player interactions, and cookies or similar technologies under their policies.

ReBailamos stores the selected video ID, title, duration, start and end points, playback speed, repeat settings, and your consent choice locally on your device. ReBailamos does not request access to your Google Account, receive your YouTube credentials, download or modify YouTube media, enable background YouTube playback, or include YouTube audio or video in saved clips. Saved clips contain only the camera recording and room microphone.

You can remove the selected YouTube video in ReBailamos or delete the app to remove locally stored YouTube settings. You can also review services connected to your Google Account on Google’s security permissions page; ReBailamos does not currently request a Google Account connection.

Optional feedback

If you submit feedback in the app, ReBailamos sends the category, message, app version and build, and iOS version through Firebase. Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous service identifier, and Firebase App Check verifies that the request came from the genuine app. The feedback is delivered to the developer’s private GitHub issue tracker.

Delivery and rate-limit metadata in Firebase is scheduled for deletion after 30 days. The feedback message remains in the private issue tracker until it is no longer needed or you request its deletion. Feedback is optional and never includes your recordings or imported practice media unless you deliberately describe them in your message.

Apple services

If you choose Apple Music, Apple processes your authorization and playback requests under its own privacy policy. If you purchase ReBailamos Pro, Apple processes the transaction and provides the app with subscription status. We do not receive your payment details.

Storage and deletion

App settings and imported practice media remain in ReBailamos on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Clips previously saved to Photos must be deleted separately from the Photos app.

You can revoke camera, microphone, Photos, or Apple Music access at any time in iOS Settings.

To request deletion of feedback or other data held by the developer, use the contact method below. Deleting the app does not automatically delete feedback that you previously submitted.

Children’s privacy

ReBailamos does not knowingly collect personal information from children. YouTube videos designated as Made for Kids are not supported by the YouTube feature.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.
